# Privacy Policy & SMS Terms of Service

**Last Updated:** November 2026

## 1. Privacy Policy
We value your privacy. Mobile phone numbers collected for this service are used exclusively to deliver automated system status, IoT telemetry, and emergency alerts.

**Mobile Information Sharing:**
No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

## 2. SMS Terms & Conditions
* **Program Description:** Automated event notifications, home security alerts, and system health status.
* **Message Frequency:** Message frequency varies based on system triggers and alerts.
* **Cost:** Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.
* **Opt-Out:** You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Simply text **STOP** to unsubscribe.
* **Help:** For assistance, reply with **HELP** or contact system administrator support.
